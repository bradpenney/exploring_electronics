# Power

Every circuit on this site needs a steady voltage from somewhere. This topic covers both ends of that: the chemistry that stores energy in a battery, and the regulators and supplies that turn a sagging battery or a wall outlet into the exact voltage a circuit wants.

The articles assume [Circuit Foundations](circuit_foundations.md), especially [Ohm's Law and Power](ohms_law.md); the regulators article also leans on [Diodes and LEDs](diodes_and_leds.md) and [Transistors](transistors.md).
