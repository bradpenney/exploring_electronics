# Practical Tools

The topic articles teach electronics; these pages cover the equipment used along the way. They're a reference shelf rather than a course: read each one when a project first needs it.

## On the Shelf

- **[Breadboards](tools/breadboards.md)** — how the holes connect inside, how the power rails work, and the wiring mistakes that stop a first circuit before it starts
- **[arduino-cli](tools/arduino_cli.md)** — compiling and uploading Arduino code from the command line, and reading the board's serial output
- **[Using a Multimeter](tools/multimeter.md)** — why the jacks matter more than the dial, what the fuse and CAT rating protect, ranges and accuracy, and choosing a meter
- **[Bench Power Supplies](tools/bench_power_supply.md)** — constant voltage, constant current, the crossover, a safe first power-up, and reading the spec sheet
- **[Soldering](tools/soldering.md)** — why solder bonds only to clean, hot metal, judging a joint, choosing solder and temperature, desoldering, and fume and lead safety

The multimeter page covers the meter itself; each measuring article in [Circuit Foundations](circuit_foundations.md) teaches its own setting: [voltage](voltage.md#measuring-voltage), [current](current.md#measuring-current), [resistance](resistance.md#measuring-resistance), and [continuity](open_short_fuses.md#continuity-testing-for-a-complete-path).

## Where to Start

Building your first circuit? Start with **[Breadboards](tools/breadboards.md)**. Ready to program a board? **[arduino-cli](tools/arduino_cli.md)** gets your first sketch onto it. Bought a meter? Read **[Using a Multimeter](tools/multimeter.md)** before its first measurement.
